# Data Availability

The underlying training data are not publicly released.

The data consist of private human-AI chat histories and contain project-defining interaction patterns. To preserve personal confidentiality, authorial control, and the conceptual integrity of the research design, neither raw nor curated training examples are deposited in a public, restricted, gated, or otherwise access-controlled repository.

Instead, this repository provides aggregate dataset statistics, schema definitions, preprocessing documentation, synthetic rejected-response protocols, tool-call augmentation procedures, and validation criteria.

This decision follows the principle of data minimization: for preregistration and methodological transparency, it is necessary to document the selection, segmentation, generation, and validation procedures; disclosure of private chat content is neither necessary nor proportionate.
